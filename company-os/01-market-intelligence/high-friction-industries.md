# High-Friction Industries (Local Priority)

Prioritize where margins are decent, operations are chaotic, existing software is weak, owners are busy, and work is repetitive.

## Strong targets (Cedar Rapids and surrounding)

- HVAC
- Plumbing
- Roofing
- Concrete
- Landscaping
- Home cleaning
- Auto repair
- Towing
- Trucking / logistics
- Small manufacturers
- Dental offices
- Veterinarians
- Restoration
- Property management
- Nonprofits
- Local distributors
- Field-service companies generally

## Sweet spot

**Companies with about 5–50 employees** — spreadsheets break, enterprise is overkill, owners still decide quickly.
